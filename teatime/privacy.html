Privacy Policy

Jigsaw Puzzles: Teatime HD (“the game”) is made by ASJK.
Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. The game works offline. Ads in the game are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some device information as described below.

Information the game keeps on your phone

Your puzzle progress, coins, settings and finished pictures are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. If you uninstall the game, this information is removed.

Your own photos

If you use “Make your own puzzle”, the photo you pick is copied into the game on your device so you can play it. Your photos are never uploaded or shared. The game uses the Android photo picker, so it can only see the photo you choose.

New pictures

When your phone is online, the game may download new puzzle pictures from our website (asjkproject.github.io). Only the pictures are downloaded; no personal information is sent.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. In some regions (for example the UK and the European Economic Area) you will be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases such as “Remove ads” and picture packs are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
