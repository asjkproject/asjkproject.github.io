Golf Card Game: Par Six — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Golf Card Game: Par Six ("the app") is an offline card game made by ASJK. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There is no login and no account. Your game progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address) and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. This is handled by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows one daily reminder. It is created on your device and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
