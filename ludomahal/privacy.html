Privacy Policy — Ludo Mahal

Developer: ASJK · Contact: asjk.apps@gmail.com

Ludo Mahal is a game made by ASJK. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Ludo Mahal does not ask for an account, a login, your email or your phone number, and it does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (coins, level, records, daily reward days, dice you unlocked), your settings, a game you left unfinished, and any player names you type are saved only on your own phone, so the game can remember them. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Ads and other companies

This version has no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will say which company provides the ads and what that company collects.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone. Because we collect nothing, we do not knowingly collect information from children.
