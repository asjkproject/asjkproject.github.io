Glowtank – Privacy Policy

App: Glowtank (Android) · Developer: ASJK (Amit Verma) · Effective 16 November 2026

Short version

Glowtank has no account and no ads, no tracking. Everything you enter — tanks, tests, photos, expenses — is stored only on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Phone backup

If backup is on in your phone settings, Android may keep a copy of your Glowtank data in your own Google account. This is a feature of your phone. We never see it.

Internet

Glowtank works without the internet. It does not upload your tanks, tests or photos anywhere.

Backups

When you tap "Save backup file", the file goes where you choose. Android may also include app data in your own Google device backup, if you have it turned on.

Sharing

When you tap "Share tank card", Glowtank makes a picture on your phone. It goes only to the app you choose, like WhatsApp.

Purchases

Glowtank Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details.

Permissions

Notifications: to show your reminders. Photos: only the pictures you pick.

Not veterinary advice

Target ranges and tips in Glowtank are general guidance from common aquarium guides. They are not advice from a vet. For sick fish, ask a vet or an experienced fish store.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on this phone. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Glowtank, removes all of it. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

Glowtank is made for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here and on the app's store page. Effective 16 November 2026.
